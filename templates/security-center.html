Protect Yourself Online

Best practices to keep your accounts secure

✅ Do These Things

Use Strong Passwords

12+ characters with letters, numbers, and symbols. Use different passwords for each account.

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Add an extra layer of security with SMS codes or authenticator apps.

Monitor Your Accounts

Check statements regularly and set up account alerts for transactions.

Keep Software Updated

Update your browser, operating system, and mobile apps regularly.

Use Secure Networks

Avoid public Wi-Fi for banking. Use your mobile data or secure home network.

❌ Avoid These Mistakes

Never Share Login Info

We'll never ask for passwords, PINs, or security codes via email or phone.

Don't Click Suspicious Links

Always type our website URL directly or use bookmarks. Verify email sources.

Avoid Public Computers

Don't access banking on shared or public computers in libraries, cafes, etc.

Don't Save Passwords

Avoid saving banking passwords in browsers on shared or work computers.

Watch for Phishing

Be wary of urgent emails asking for account information or threatening account closure.

Common Fraud Types

Know what to look for and how to respond

Phishing Emails

Fake emails designed to steal your login credentials or personal information.

Red flags: Urgent language, suspicious links, requests for passwords

Identity Theft

Criminals using your personal information to open accounts or make purchases.

Red flags: Unknown accounts, unexpected bills, credit score changes

Card Skimming

Devices that steal card information when you swipe at ATMs or card readers.

Red flags: Loose card readers, unusual keypad behavior, unexpected charges
