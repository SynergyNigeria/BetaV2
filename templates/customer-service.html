Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I reset my online banking password?

You can reset your password directly from your dashboard login screen, or contact our support team for immediate assistance.

What should I do if I suspect fraudulent activity?

Immediately contact us at 1-800-BETA-BANK or use the fraud reporting feature in your dashboard. We monitor accounts 24/7 for suspicious activity.

How can I increase my credit limit?

Submit a credit limit increase request through your card management dashboard, or speak with a representative who can review your account.

Can I make loan payments online?

Yes! All loan payments can be made through your online dashboard, including setting up automatic payments and viewing payment history.
