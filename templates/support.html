Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

You can reset your password by clicking on "Forgot Password" on the login page. We'll send you a secure link to reset your password via email.

How long does a transfer take?

Internal transfers are instant. External transfers typically take 1-3 business days depending on the receiving bank and transfer method.

What are your fees?

We offer transparent pricing with no hidden fees. Check our fee schedule in your account settings or contact support for detailed information.

Is my money safe with BetaBank?

Yes, your money is protected by FDIC insurance up to $250,000. We also use bank-level security and encryption to protect your data and transactions.
